A note for my Chotu · always

My dear Chotu,

I don't know if I tell you enough, but I am genuinely so grateful that you're part of my life. Out of all the people we could meet in this huge world, somehow our paths crossed — and that means something to me.

You don't have to be cheerful all the time. You don't have to make everyone laugh, have everything figured out, or hide the parts of you that feel tired. You are allowed to have quiet days, confusing days, messy days, and days when you just need someone to sit beside you without asking you to explain everything.

I hope you know that your worth doesn't disappear when you make a mistake, when you feel lost, or when you don't feel like your best self. You are still you. Still important. Still deserving of gentleness, patience, and good things.

And if I could wish one thing for you, it would be that you could see yourself through kinder eyes. I wish you could see the little things that make you special, the ways you leave a mark on people's lives, and all the beautiful things that are still waiting for you.

Thank you for the memories we've already made, for the laughs, and for simply being someone I care about this much. I can't promise I'll always know the perfect thing to say, but I can promise that your feelings matter to me and that I will keep trying to show up with care.

Whenever life gets too loud, come back to this little page. Let it remind you that you don't have to carry every hard thing alone. One day, one breath, one tiny step at a time, okay?

Always in your corner,
Vanish ♡

open on a difficult day

When the world feels too heavy

You don't have to explain why you're sad before you deserve comfort. You don't have to fix everything tonight. You're allowed to pause, breathe, ask for help, and take things slowly.

Chotu, listen to me for a tiny moment. A hard day is not proof that you're failing. Feeling overwhelmed doesn't make you weak, and needing support doesn't make you a burden. You are a person, not a machine — you deserve rest, care, and room to be imperfect.

Please do one small kind thing for yourself. Have some water. Unclench your shoulders. Take a few slow breaths. Message someone you trust if being alone with your thoughts feels like too much. You don't have to solve your whole life in one night.

I hope you remember that there are still soft mornings ahead, songs that will make you smile, people who will care, and memories you haven't made yet. For now, just take the next little step. I'm cheering for you, always.

You're precious to me. Don't forget that. ♡ — Vanish
